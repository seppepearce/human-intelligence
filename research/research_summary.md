## ⚠️ Critical Success Factors

### **Must-Have Capabilities**
1. **Intuitive Branching UX** - make Git workflows accessible to non-developers
2. **Beautiful Visual Trees** - knowledge representation must be inspiring
3. **Seamless Real-time Collaboration** - multiple users editing without friction
4. **Community Quality Control** - democratic curation with high standards

### **Key Risks to Mitigate**
1. **Complex mental model** - extensive UX testing and onboarding required
2. **Network effects cold start** - need seed content and expert contributors
3. **Big Tech competition** - build defensible community and first-mover advantage

## 🚀 Go-to-Market Strategy

### **Phase 1: Developer Community Validation (Months 1-6)**
- **Target**: 1,000 technical users familiar with Git workflows
- **Strategy**: Launch in developer communities (Hacker News, Reddit r/programming)
- **Goal**: Prove branching/merging works for knowledge
- **Success Metric**: 60%+ weekly retention, 100+ successful knowledge merges

### **Phase 2: Academic Expansion (Months 6-12)**
- **Target**: University researchers and graduate students  
- **Strategy**: Partner with progressive universities, academic conferences
- **Goal**: Establish credibility in knowledge management space
- **Success Metric**: 5+ institutional partnerships, research citations

### **Phase 3: Professional Scale (Months 12-18)**
- **Target**: Corporate knowledge workers and teams
- **Strategy**: Bottom-up adoption, enterprise pilot programs
- **Goal**: Scale to sustainable business model
- **Success Metric**: $1M+ ARR, 50+ enterprise customers
