# 🏛️ Digital Agora Prototype - Demonstration Guide

> **Experience ancient Greek forum dynamics in digital form**  
> **Key Concept**: Natural constraints enhance discourse, they don't limit it

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### **1. Start the Prototype**
```bash
cd agora-prototype
./start.sh
```

### **2. Open Your Browser**
Navigate to: **http://localhost:3001**

### **3. You're Now in the Digital Agora!**
- You'll appear as "Socrates" (🧠) by default
- See the plaza with active discussion spaces
- Notice your friends "Plato" and "Aristotle" exploring different areas

---

## 🎯 What This Prototype Demonstrates

### **Core Agora Principles**

#### **1. Natural Size Constraints**
- Each discussion space caps at **12 participants**
- When full, you're gently redirected to related spaces
- **Watch for**: Spaces showing "12/12 participants" with red indicators

#### **2. Always-Visible Presence**
- No "invisible mode" - you're either present or logged out
- Friends can see where you're exploring
- **Watch for**: "Your Circle's Wanderings" section showing friend activity

#### **3. Cross-Pollination Through Crowding**
- When your usual spaces get crowded, discover new territories
- **Watch for**: "Explore New Territory" suggestions

#### **4. Friend Network Discovery**
- See where friends are and join them in unfamiliar spaces
- **Watch for**: Blue "Join →" buttons next to friend locations

---

## 🌊 Key Behaviors to Observe

### **The Crowded Circle Effect**
1. **Notice spaces marked as "full" (red indicators)**
2. **Try to join a full space** - you'll get alternative suggestions

### **Friend Following Dynamics**
1. **Look at "Your Circle's Wanderings"** - see where Plato and Aristotle are

2. **See activity levels fluctuate** - green (very active) to gray (quiet)
3. **Notice suggestions adapt** as spaces fill and empty

### **Cross-Pollination Opportunities**
1. **Check "Explore New Territory"** - spaces outside your usual domains
2. **Notice the purple highlighting** - indicates unfamiliar but related areas
3. **Try exploring** - see how it might lead to unexpected insights

## 🔍 What to Look For

### **Visual Indicators**

#### **Space Capacity Bars**
- **Green**: Plenty of room (0-60% full)
- **Blue**: Getting active (60-80% full)  
- **Yellow**: Quite busy (80-90% full)
- **Red**: At capacity (90-100% full)

#### **Activity Levels**
- **Very High**: Intense green border, lots of participants
- **High**: Yellow border, good discussion flow
- **Medium**: Blue border, steady conversation
- **Low**: Gray border, quieter spaces

#### **Friend Presence**
- **Cyan highlights**: Spaces where friends are active
- **"Join →" buttons**: Direct links to join friends

### **Suggestion Types**

#### **Recommended for You** (Yellow section)
- Spaces aligned with your interests
- Friends present get priority
- Based on topics you usually explore

#### **Explore New Territory** (Purple section)  
- Spaces outside your usual domains
- Cross-pollination opportunities
- Intellectual adventure suggestions
