Knowledge is not linear. It branches, it grows, it evolves through collaboration. Yet most of us seem trapped in solitary confinement, in tools that bottleneck our thinking, or do it for us. Human Intelligence aims to shift this paradigm by building the first true collaborative knowledge ecosystem—where ideas take root, grow through community, and flourish into deeper understanding and insight. 

## The problems faced
- Information is everywhere. Understanding is nowhere. How can we build a system that maximises knowledge throughput? 
- Today's tools fail us: Linear documents can't capture the true complexity of ideas. 
- Quality information is often withheld from the mainstream, while most readily available information warps reality in some fashion. Either by human error, or worse, deceit.
- Want to learn something new or deepen your understanding on a particular topic? Good luck not going down rabbit holes and coming out the other end not a minute wiser. What did you actually learn, did you even remember the next day? What's that? Well yes I'm talking about you!

## The *agora* of the future 
The Greeks had it right the first time around, probably. How can we find the middle ground for respectful discourse and generating interesting thought, while simultaneously avoiding both censorship and degeneracy? The answer: [[Sociology]]

## Keeping our eyes on the road 
During development we opt for an iterative approach rather than building everything, everywhere, all at once. That's not to say we haven't pondered about the repercussions of a platform like this. Actors tend to use, misuse and abuse platforms like this in all sorts of ways the creators didn't foresee. Therefore we will try to avoid the most common pitfalls by defining our roadmap, features and policies early on, rather than trying to put out the fire when it gets to our doorstep. Take a look at [[Feature set - MVP & road map]] for our MVP and full feature set.

Possible use cases could be: 
- Personal projects / second brain. You could also just build out your own private knowledge if you dont wish to share it with others.
- Private use in a classroom, for students to collaborate on subject matter. 
- News stories, A storyline could develop and nodes could be fact checked / voted on 
* Just all around having fun developing and sharing knowledge together with other people about esoteric things 
* Research, tree structures and nodes could be combined to create new insights
* Potential employers looking for knowledgeable people in certain fields
* Educators could create their own roadmaps to follow in an interactive way
