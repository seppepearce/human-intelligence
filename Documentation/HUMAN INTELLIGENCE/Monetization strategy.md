Important: These figures are obviously not set in stone, this document serves as a general wireframe of how the app could be monetized.

**Free Tier:**
- Unlimited collaboration
- Basic search and discovery
- Up to 10 owned trees public or private
- Private collaboration up to 3 people

**Creator Plan ($7/month):**

- Unlimited trees
- Access to AI features 
- Analytics on tree engagement
- Monetization tools (paid access to premium trees)
- Priority support
- Private collaboration trees up to 10 people

**Organization Institution / Plan ($49/month + per-user):**

- Private organization workspaces
- Collaboration up to 30+ users.
- Advanced tools
- Admin controls and user management
- Custom branding options
- Usage analytics dashboard


**Educators Program:**
- Educators could sign up to the platform to create trees that are served as high quality educational content. They can sell access to these trees, and we will take a fee on every sale.
