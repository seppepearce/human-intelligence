Today's knowledge tools fail us:

The human mind doesn't think in documents. It thinks in connections, in trees of possibility, in branching paths of understanding.

The Solution: Living Knowledge Trees
Human Intelligence is a collaborative platform where knowledge grows organically through community cultivation.
Plant a Seed
Any user can plant a seed—a root node containing an idea, question, or piece of knowledge in rich markdown format.
Grow the Tree
From each node, paths branch outward. Build linearly to develop an idea further. Branch sideways to explore alternatives, counterarguments, or related concepts.
Collaborate Naturally
Public trees invite collaboration. Users branch from any node, develop their contribution, then propose merging their insights back into the main knowledge tree.
Navigate Intuitively
Experience your knowledge trees through our dungeon-style exploration interface. Choose your path through the branching structure, discovering new connections with each journey.
Distill Understanding
Every node supports community-generated TL;DR summaries. The best rise to the top through voting, helping newcomers quickly grasp complex ideas.

Use Cases
Academic Excellence
Transform classrooms into knowledge workshops. Students collaborate on subject trees, building understanding together. Teachers guide growth while students contribute insights, creating living textbooks that evolve with each cohort.
Truth in Media
News stories become knowledge trees. Facts branch into verification, context, and analysis. Community fact-checking happens at the node level. Misinformation withers when better information branches grow stronger.
Research Acceleration
Researchers build upon each other's work visually. Hypothesis branches compete, evidence accumulates at nodes, and breakthrough insights emerge from unexpected connections between trees.
Cultural Knowledge
Communities preserve and grow their collective wisdom. Traditional knowledge, local insights, and cultural understanding find structure and permanence while remaining alive and evolving.

Design Philosophy
Academic Neutrality
Clean, minimal interface prioritizing content over chrome. Think research library, not social media. Typography and spacing that honors the knowledge it contains.
Visual Intelligence
The tree visualization is where we transcend. Elegant node design, smooth branching animations, intuitive navigation controls. Users should feel the structure of knowledge, not just read it.
Organic Growth
No artificial constraints on tree structure. Knowledge grows as it needs to grow. Our interface adapts to the natural shape of understanding.
